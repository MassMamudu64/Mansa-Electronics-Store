08 — Logo Usage

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • ✓ Place on white, warm-paper, or espresso (brown-900).
  • ✓ Keep clear space = cap-height of the "M" on all sides.
  • ✓ Brown-on-light, gold/white-on-dark only.
  • ✓ Use the icon (Africa silhouette) for favicons ≥16px.

✕ Don't

  • ✕ Recolor the wordmark or flatten gold to one tone.
  • ✕ Stretch, skew, rotate, or add shadows/glows.
  • ✕ Place the full lockup on busy photos.
  • ✕ Put gold wordmark on light gold/sand (low contrast).
